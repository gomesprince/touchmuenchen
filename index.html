About Touch

What is Touch Rugby?

Forget what you might think about traditional rugby. Touch Rugby is a fast-paced, minimal-contact sport that's all about speed, strategy and teamwork. It's designed to be safe, fun and accessible for absolutely everyone.

The rules are simple: a "tackle" is just a simple touch on the person with the ball. There are no scrums and no collisions. It's a dynamic game that's incredibly easy to learn.

Our community

A Group for Everyone

Our community brings together people from across Munich and beyond. We are a friendly, international group united by a shared enjoyment of Touch Rugby, exercise and meeting new people.

  • Age or gender: We are a fully mixed-gender group with members of different ages.
  • Fitness level: The game can be played at different paces, making it suitable for a wide range of fitness levels.
  • Experience: Many of our members had never played before joining. Beginners are always welcome, and we're happy to teach new players the game.

Our Activities

Social Sunday Games

The heart of our group. We meet every Sunday at Werneckwiese in the Englischer Garten for casual, friendly games — a great way to learn the rules, get some exercise and socialize.

Training Sessions

For those looking to improve their skills, passing and understanding of the game's strategy, we also offer optional training sessions.
