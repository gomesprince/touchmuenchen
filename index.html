About us

Social Touch Rugby for everyone.

Touch München is a friendly community of Touch Rugby players in Munich. We welcome experienced players, newcomers and anyone who wants to try the sport in a relaxed and social environment.

Touch is a fast, non-contact form of rugby focused on movement, teamwork and fun. Our sessions are mixed and open to all levels.
